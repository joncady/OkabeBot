    NFC tags

    Tap a tag to call the companion or mark a recurring chore done. First write the tag using Home Assistant’s Android app → Settings → Tags. No credentials are written to the sticker.

    Uses the tag’s HA area and the screen room bindings in Amadeus setup. A scan may wake/cast to a TV and replace its current app. No home/away inference or continuous tracking; the scanner’s identity is unknown. Automatic routing prefers a screen in the tag’s area. If that area has no screen, you may explicitly choose a fallback in another area. If multiple screens share the area, choose one of them.

    Each scan marks this chore done and starts its next delay. Overdue reminders respect your Amadeus boundaries. Turning off the tag does not pause the chore.
